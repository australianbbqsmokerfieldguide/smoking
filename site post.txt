## Low & Slow — Australian BBQ Smoker Field Guide

I’ve been putting together an Australian-focused BBQ smoker field guide and wanted to share it with the BBQ community.

It’s designed as a practical reference for cooking low and slow, covering:

* Brisket, chuck roast, beef ribs, pulled pork and ribs so far
* Smoker and internal meat temperatures
* Cook times, resting and holding
* Dry rub recipes
* Smoking wood flavour guide
* Step-by-step smoking methods
* BBQ sides and recipes
* Smoker and BBQ equipment reviews
* °C / °F temperature conversion

The site can also be **installed like an app on your phone or tablet**, allowing you to use the guide **offline without an internet connection** while you’re out back in the bush.

The aim is to keep it simple and useful while you’re actually cooking, rather than filling it with unnecessary theory.

I’m still building it, so feedback, corrections and suggestions are very welcome.

https://australianbbqsmokerfieldguide.github.io/smoking/
